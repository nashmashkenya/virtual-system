# ElimuPawa Classroom Teacher Quick Guide (One Page)

Use this as a simple class checklist. Designed for non-technical teachers.

---

## 1) Before Class (1 Minute)

- Sign in to your **Teacher meeting room**.
- Confirm:
  - `Camera on`
  - `Mic on`
  - `Chat on` (if you want student messages)
  - `Hands on` (if you want raise-hand requests)
- Open **Learners** tab and confirm students are visible.

---

## 2) Main Controls You Need

- **Camera on/off**: control your video.
- **Mic on/off**: control your audio.
- **Chat on/off**: allow or stop student chat.
- **Slow mode**: reduce chat spam.
- **Hands on/off**: allow or stop raise-hand requests.
- **Learners**: manage students and waiting room.
- **Activities**: attendance, Q&A, polls, quizzes, ops snapshot.
- **Leave**: end your participation in class.

Tip: Use **Simple view** for normal teaching. Use **More controls** only when needed.

---

## 3) During Class

Every few minutes:

1. Check **Learners** for waiting students.
2. Check **Activities** for raised hands and Q&A.
3. Keep the room focused:
   - Turn **Slow mode** on if chat is noisy.
   - Turn **Chat** or **Hands** off temporarily if needed.

---

## 4) Waiting Room (If Enabled)

- In **Learners** tab:
  - Click **Approve** to admit a student.
  - Click **Deny** to reject a request.

If no students appear, waiting room may be off.

---

## 5) Q&A and Moderation

- Open **Activities** to review class questions.
- Use:
  - **Publish all** to show queued questions.
  - **Dismiss all** to clear queued questions.

---

## 6) Breakouts (Optional)

Use only if you need group work:

- Click **More controls**.
- Open breakout rooms.
- Set timer.
- Broadcast instructions.
- Close breakouts to return everyone.

If you do not need breakout rooms, ignore this section.

---

## 7) YouTube Broadcast Setup Checklist (For Large Classes)

Use this when Delivery mode is set to **Broadcast**.

1. Open YouTube Studio and schedule/start your live stream.
2. Paste the YouTube watch/live link into **YouTube Live link** in Class setup.
3. Start stream in YouTube 5-10 minutes before class.
4. Keep students on ElimuPawa Classroom for chat, Q&A, waiting room, and learner management.
5. If video drops, restart in YouTube Studio; keep interaction running in ElimuPawa Classroom.

---

## 8) Quick Troubleshooting

- Refresh page: `Ctrl + F5`
- If students cannot chat: check **Chat on** and **Slow mode**
- If students cannot raise hands: check **Hands on**
- If screen seems outdated: sign out and sign in again

---

## 9) End Class

1. Give final instructions.
2. Optionally turn **Chat off**.
3. Click **Leave**.
