1 Creating your teacher account

ElimuPawa uses a secure sign-in system for teachers. You can sign in with your email address or a Google account.

  1. Go to virtual-system.replit.app/teacher/sign-in
  2. Click "Sign up" if you don't have an account yet
  3. Enter your email and create a password — or click "Continue with Google" for one-tap sign-in
  4. Once signed in you land on the Teacher Dashboard — your control centre
💡Use the same email every time you sign in. Students will be assigned to your classes by subject, not by teacher name.

2 Setting up classes & subjects

Before scheduling lessons you need at least one class. A class ties together a subject, a class level, and a group of students.

  1. From the dashboard click "Classes" in the left sidebar (or top nav on mobile)
  2. Click "New Class"
  3. Fill in: Subject (e.g. Mathematics), Class Level (e.g. Form 3), and an optional class name
  4. Click "Create Class" — it appears in your classes list immediately
💡You can create multiple classes for the same subject at different levels — e.g. "Maths Form 2" and "Maths Form 4" as separate classes.
📌If the subject or class level you need is not in the list, ask your school administrator to add it in the Admin panel.

3 Scheduling lessons

Lessons are the individual sessions students join. Each lesson belongs to one of your classes.

  1. Open a class and click "Schedule Lesson"
  2. Enter the lesson title, date, and start time
  3. Set the duration (default 60 minutes)
  4. Click "Save" — the lesson is now visible to enrolled students
📅

Upcoming lessons

Students see scheduled lessons on their dashboard and can prepare in advance.

✏️

Editing a lesson

Click the lesson title and select "Edit" to change time, title, or duration.

🗑️

Cancelling a lesson

Open the lesson and click "Delete". Students will no longer see it on their dashboard.

4 Managing students

Students register themselves using their ADM number and parent phone. Once they register, you can approve them for specific lessons.

Viewing students

Go to Classes → your class → Students tab to see all students who have requested to join your lessons.

Approving students for a lesson

  1. Open a lesson and go to the Students section
  2. Students who sent a join request appear with a Pending badge
  3. Click "Approve" next to each student — they can now enter the live classroom
💡You can approve all students at once using the "Approve all" button at the top of the list.
⚠️Students who are not approved will see a "Waiting for approval" message when they try to join. They cannot enter the classroom until you approve them.

5 Running a live classroom

When it's time for your lesson, open the live classroom from your dashboard.

  1. Click "Start Lesson" on the scheduled lesson card
  2. The Teacher Room opens — you see your student roster, chat, and all controls
  3. Students who are approved will be able to join from their dashboard
  4. When you're done, click "End Lesson" to close the session for everyone

6 Classroom tools

📊 Polls

Use polls to quickly check understanding or gather opinions.

  1. In the classroom, open the Polls panel from the toolbar
  2. Type your question and add 2–4 answer options
  3. Click "Launch Poll" — all students see it immediately on their screen
  4. Watch results update live as students vote
  5. Click "Close Poll" when done — results stay visible to you

📝 Quizzes

Run a graded quiz at any point during the lesson.

  1. Open the Quiz panel and click "New Quiz"
  2. Add one or more questions with multiple-choice answers — mark the correct answer for each
  3. Click "Launch Quiz" — students answer within the time limit you set
  4. Results show each student's score when the quiz closes

🖊️ Whiteboard

Draw diagrams, work through problems, and annotate content in real time.

  1. Click the Whiteboard tab in the classroom
  2. Use the pen, shapes, and text tools to write or draw
  3. Students see your whiteboard live — you can also allow them to draw on it
  4. Click "Clear" to reset the board, or "Save" to keep a snapshot
💡The whiteboard works great for maths equations, diagrams, and brainstorming. On a touchscreen device you can draw with your finger.

7 Live video & YouTube streaming

Browser webcam (simplest)

If your device has a camera, ElimuPawa can use it directly in the browser — no app needed.

  1. Click "Start Video" in the classroom toolbar
  2. Allow the browser to access your camera and microphone when prompted
  3. Your video stream becomes visible to all students in the classroom

YouTube Live (recommended for large classes)

For better quality or when teaching more than 30 students, stream via YouTube Live and paste the link into the classroom.

  1. Open YouTube Studio on your phone or computer and start a Live stream
  2. Copy your stream URL or video ID
  3. In the ElimuPawa classroom, click "YouTube Live" in the video panel
  4. Paste the link and click "Go Live" — students see your YouTube stream inside the classroom
📱On Android, use the YouTube app → tap your profile photo → "Go Live" to start streaming from your phone camera in seconds.
⚠️YouTube Live requires your channel to be verified. If you haven't streamed before, there may be a 24-hour activation wait the first time.

8 Tips & best practices

Before the lesson

  • Schedule the lesson at least a day before so students can see it on their dashboards
  • Approve all expected students before the lesson starts to avoid delays
  • Test your camera/microphone beforehand — use a different browser tab to check
  • Prepare any polls or quiz questions in advance so you can launch them quickly

During the lesson

  • Start with a quick poll to check attendance and warm up students
  • Use the whiteboard for worked examples — students follow along much better visually
  • Check the chat panel regularly — students often ask questions there
  • Use breakout rooms for group tasks of 5–10 minutes, then bring everyone back
  • Run a short quiz at the end to check understanding before closing

After the lesson

  • Review quiz scores from the lesson summary — students who scored below 50% may need follow-up
  • Post any notes or resources in the class materials section for students to review
  • Schedule the next lesson promptly so students can plan ahead
🚀Need help? Contact your school administrator or reach out to the ElimuPawa support team from the Settings page.
